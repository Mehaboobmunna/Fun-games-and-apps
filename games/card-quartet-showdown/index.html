How to Play (Card Quartet)

🎯 The Goal: Be the first player to collect all 4 cards of the same kind (e.g. 4 Lions, 4 Inceptions, or 4 Mars cards).
  1. The Deal: 4 cards of each suit are created for every player (e.g. 4 players = 16 cards). Every player starts with 4 cards.
  2. Dealer Passes First: The dealer selects 1 card they don't want and passes it secretly to the next player clockwise.
  3. Holding 5 Cards: The next player receives this card and now holds 5 cards. They choose 1 of their 5 cards to pass to the next player.
  4. Continuous Passing: This continues around the circle until someone collects all 4 cards of a single suit.
  5. Secrecy: Cards are passed face-down so other players cannot see what you are collecting!
Scoring Rules:
• Round Winner: Scores 100 points.
• Other Players: Score 25 points per card in their single highest matching set (e.g., 3 matching = 75 pts, 2 matching = 50 pts).
